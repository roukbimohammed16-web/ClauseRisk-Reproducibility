1. CONVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Placeholders are written in braces: {lang_name} is "English", "French" or
  "Arabic" according to the detected language of the passage; {text} is the
  passage to classify; {ctx} is the list of retrieved knowledge-base clauses.
- Each retrieved clause is rendered on one line as:
      [<clause id>] (risk=<low|medium|high>, cat=<risk category>): <clause text
      in the language of the passage, falling back to English>
- In the evaluation pipeline of every version (v1-v5), the model returns a
  single JSON field:  {"overall_risk": "none|low|medium|high"}
  (OpenAI JSON mode, response_format = {"type": "json_object"}).
  An unparsable answer, or a value outside the four levels, is mapped to "low".
- Passages are truncated to 1,500 characters (v1) or 1,200 characters (v2 and
  later) in the user prompt; retrieved clauses to 250 (v1) or 200 characters.

3. v2 — HyDE + hybrid retrieval + few-shot + self-consistency (3 votes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 HyDE query expansion
Model: gpt-4o-mini | temperature 0.3 | max_tokens 80 | plain text
The generated clause is appended to the passage, and the combined text is used
as the query for both BM25 and dense retrieval (RRF, k = 60, top 10).

[USER]
Given the following user clause in {lang_name}, write a SHORT hypothetical legal
clause (1-2 sentences max) that would describe the same type of risk, using
formal legal terminology. Output ONLY the hypothetical clause text, nothing else.

User clause: {text[:500]}

Hypothetical legal clause:

3.2 Classification (one vote)
Model: gpt-4o-mini | temperature 0.3 | max_tokens 50 | JSON mode

[SYSTEM]
You are an expert multilingual legal risk analyst specializing in Algerian law
(Loi 18-07 data protection, Loi 09-03 consumer protection, Loi 18-05 e-commerce).

CRITICAL CLASSIFICATION RULES:
- HIGH: Direct violation of Algerian law OR explicit waiver of rights OR
  international data transfer without ANPDP authorization OR abusive clause
  forbidden by Loi 09-03
- MEDIUM: Potential violation OR ambiguous clause OR insufficient transparency
  OR unilateral modification
- LOW: Standard compliant clause OR purely descriptive content
- NONE: No legal content

Be DECISIVE. When in doubt between MEDIUM and HIGH on cumulative violations,
prefer HIGH (precautionary principle).
Respond in {lang_name}. Output ONLY valid JSON.

[USER]
{FEW_SHOT_BLOCK}
NOW CLASSIFY THIS USER TEXT:

User clause: {text}

Retrieved similar clauses from knowledge base:
{ctx}

Apply the same reasoning as the examples above. Output ONLY: {"overall_risk": "none|low|medium|high"}

3.3 FEW_SHOT_BLOCK (fixed across all runs; used from v2 onward)
Selection rule: the first two HIGH entries, the first two MEDIUM entries and the
first LOW entry outside the "no match" category, in knowledge-base order:
  1. cl_00055 (HIGH)
  2. cl_00057 (HIGH)
  3. cl_00002 (MEDIUM)
  4. cl_00006 (MEDIUM)
  5. cl_00053 (LOW)

EXAMPLES (study these carefully):

Example 1:
Clause: We can transfer national security data to third parties as needed.
Reasoning: Violation: Espionage/treason exposure (sovereign/defense/economic secrets) - Legal basis: قانون العقوبات – المواد 61 إلى 67
Output: {"overall_risk": "high"}

Example 2:
Clause: We may transfer sovereign data to other countries if it is in the interests of our business parties.
Reasoning: Violation: Espionage/treason exposure (sovereign/defense/economic secrets) | Personal data protection / privacy compliance risk - Legal basis: قانون العقوبات – المواد 61 إلى 67 | قانون 18-07 (حماية المعطيات ذات الطابع الشخص
Output: {"overall_risk": "high"}

Example 3:
Clause: Disputes are interpreted in accordance with external law regardless of the location of the user.
Reasoning: Concern: Choice-of-law / forum selection evasion
Output: {"overall_risk": "medium"}

Example 4:
Clause: Any disputes between the parties shall be resolved in accordance with foreign law and not local law.
Reasoning: Concern: Choice-of-law / forum selection evasion
Output: {"overall_risk": "medium"}

Example 5:
Clause: We provide accurate geographic data to third parties outside the country without restriction.
Reasoning: Standard compliant clause
Output: {"overall_risk": "low"}

3.4 Self-consistency (v2)
Three votes: the first vote generates the HyDE expansion and performs retrieval;
votes 2 and 3 reuse the same retrieved clauses. All three votes use the
parameters of Section 3.2. Aggregation: simple majority; in case of a tie, the
more severe level is chosen (precautionary tie-break).

3.5 Class-prior adjustment (v2 only)
If the share of passages predicted HIGH is more than 10 points below a target of
0.45, MEDIUM predictions that received at least one HIGH vote are promoted to
HIGH. On EVAL-EEM this changed one prediction (3-class F1 0.593 -> 0.603).

4. v3 to v5 — five-vote GPT-4o classification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Main classifier: gpt-4o | JSON mode | output {"overall_risk": ...}
Secondary verification call: gpt-4o-mini, prompted to be skeptical of
underestimated HIGH violations.
Five votes with (temperature, perspective):
  (0.0, legal), (0.3, legal), (0.5, consumer), (0.5, privacy), (0.7, legal)
The temperature-0 vote is weighted x1.5 in the three-class aggregation
(v3-FIXED). v4 and v5 apply decision rules to the same stored votes (Section 6).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Used for (a) the three repeated runs of the pipeline and (b) the "GPT-4o without
retrieval" baseline reported in the revised manuscript.
Model: gpt-4o | max_tokens 60 | JSON mode | five votes as in Section 4.

5.1 Perspective sentences
  legal    : Reason as an Algerian legal practitioner applying the statutes literally.
  consumer : Reason from the standpoint of consumer protection (Loi 09-03).
  privacy  : Reason from the standpoint of personal-data protection (Loi 18-07).

6. DECISION STAGE (v4, v5) — no prompt involved
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Binary task: RISKY = HIGH; NOT-RISKY = NONE, LOW or MEDIUM.
Let n_HIGH be the number of HIGH votes among the K = 5 votes.
  v4               : RISKY if n_HIGH >= 3
  v5 (selected)    : RISKY if n_HIGH >= 1 OR the keyword veto fires
                     (patterns in veto_patterns.txt)
Confidence (Equation 2 of the manuscript): share of the five votes that agree
with the emitted label, i.e. n_HIGH/5 if RISKY, 1 - n_HIGH/5 if NOT-RISKY.
A prediction is emitted only if confidence >= tau; otherwise the passage is
referred to human review.

article absent from the reference framework, is rejected and the call repeated.
